## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies the regal heritage, ceremonial grandeur, and bespoke craftsmanship of haute-couture South Asian weddings. It bridges the architectural precision of Mughal symmetry with the restrained refinement of contemporary high-fashion editorial portals. The tone is authoritative, discreet, aspirational, and deeply curated.

### Target Audience
Ultra-high-net-worth diaspora and elite domestic patrons seeking couture bridal wear, master jewelers, heritage palace venues, legacy floral artisans, and royal event curators.

### Aesthetic Direction
An elevated fusion of **Refined Editorial Minimalism** and **Regal Heritage Tactility**:
- Crisp, airy structural layouts with generous architectural margins.
- Intricate, feather-light gold wirelines evoking bespoke *zardozi* threadwork rather than digital clutter.
- Immersive high-resolution photography framed in subtle ivory passe-partout borders.
- Deliberate typographic hierarchy balancing high-contrast classical serifs with whisper-quiet, tabular sans-serif metadata.
- Absence of casual digital conventions (e.g., rounded pills, garish badge colors, emojis, bouncy animations) in favor of stately transitions, calibrated opacity shifts, and deliberate pacing.

## Colors

### Color Philosophy
The palette draws direct inspiration from midnight court celebrations, uncut polki diamonds, ivory marble pavilions, and velvet ceremonial canopies.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#0B0F17` — Midnight Indigo):** Serves as the primary ground for high-drama sections, structural framing, high-contrast headings, and primary interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880` — Muted Champagne Gold):** Applied as an editorial foil—used for active indicators, hairline metallic borders, royal insignia framing, and subtle micro-highlights. Secondary rich variation: `#D4AF37` for celebratory moments and verified heirloom badges.
- **Tertiary (`#0F382C` — Deep Forest Emerald):** Reserved for curated exclusive listings, high-value bridal ateliers, verified heritage seals, and specialized luxury category accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` — Warm Alabaster):** The dominant body backdrop. Replaces cold digital whites with the warmth of handmade linen paper and unpolished marble.
- **Subtle Partition (`#E5E0D8` — Soft Slate Border):** Used for hair-thin structural divisions, grid rulers, and card outlines to preserve optical stillness without stark demarcation.

## Typography

### Structural Roles & Hierarchy
- **Editorial Voice (Playfair Display):** Handles titles, quotes, grand hero moments, and couture designer names. Its high contrast echoes classical South Asian royal proclamations rendered in western luxury typography.
- **Precision Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Governs dates, city tiers, prices in PKR/USD, appointment scheduling metadata, filter facets, and operational descriptions. It grounds the ornate serif in modern clarity.

### Usage Standards
- All section badges, status labels, and tier markers must use `label-caps` in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.15em`).
- Numeric values such as guest counts, pricing tiers, and hall capacities must render with tabular figures where applicable to maintain alignment in comparison views.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Generous 64px (`4rem`) outer margin with 32px (`2rem`) gutters. Maximum content canvas bounded at 1440px to ensure photography and typography maintain exhibition-grade proportions.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 32px outer margins with 24px gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 20px outer margin with 16px gutters.

### Rhythm & Proportions
The design relies on generous negative space to evoke an exclusive luxury salon environment. Crowding of elements is strictly prohibited; elements breathe via expansive vertical intervals. Section headers maintain a minimum separation of `space-xl` (40px) from their accompanying media and grids.

## Elevation & Depth

### Depth Philosophy
The design avoids deep, fuzzy, or synthetic drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **low-contrast outlines**, **tonal stacking**, and **fine metallic hairline borders**.

### Hierarchy of Layers
- **Base Canvas:** Flat alabaster (`#FAF8F5`) or deep midnight indigo (`#0B0F17`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Directory Cards, Panels):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or rich nightshade (`#111827`) bordered with a crisp 1px stroke of `#E5E0D8` (on light) or `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.2)` (on dark).
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Filter Bars, Concierge Drawers, Curated Lookbooks):** Frosted ambient backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at 90% opacity, framed with a 1px border of `#C5A880` at 30% opacity.
- **Shadow Profiles:** When physical separation is required (e.g., global reservation bar or luxury suite modal), use a whisper-soft ambient glow:
  - `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.05), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.03)`

## Shapes

### Shape Language: Sharp Architectural Rigor
A roundedness value of `0` is enforced across all core interactive components. Buttons, cards, images, modal sheets, and filter pills employ crisp 90-degree corners. 

This geometric austerity references classical stone-carved jaali screens, symmetrical Mughal garden parterres (*Charbagh*), and formal luxury invitation folios. Soft radiuses are reserved exclusively for inner decorative micro-embellishments or circular portrait avatars where rectangular framing is visually inappropriate.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Regal Action):** `#0B0F17` background with pure white text, 0px border radius, 1px `#0B0F17` solid border. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: transitions smoothly to `#C5A880` with dark text.
- **Secondary (Atelier Inquiry):** Transparent background with 1px `#C5A880` border. Text in `#0B0F17` (light mode) or `#C5A880` (dark mode). Subtle 0.2s gold fill reveal from bottom to top on hover.
- **Tertiary (Editorial Link):** Understated text-only link with a permanent 1px hairline border underline positioned 4px beneath the baseline. Hover: line expands from center with gold shift.

### Cards (Atelier & Heritage Venue Showcases)
- Aspect ratios: Strict 4:5 vertical portrait framing for bridal fashion, 16:10 for grand palace venues.
- Border: 1px `#E5E0D8`.
- Image interaction: High-fashion fluid zoom (`scale(1.03)`) over 700ms with an ease-out timing curve. 
- Bottom metadata pane: Warm alabaster ground with brand name in `headline-sm`, city/heritage category in `label-caps`, and price tier displayed as discreet gold numerals.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Flat, sharp rectangular tags.
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` ground, 1px `#E5E0D8` border, `#0B0F17` text in `label-caps`.
- Active: `#0B0F17` ground, `#C5A880` text, 1px `#C5A880` border.
- Hover: Border switches to 100% `#C5A880`.

### Form Fields & Inquiries
- Text Inputs: Minimalist underline or boxed input with 1px `#E5E0D8` stroke. Zero roundedness.
- Active/Focus: Border transitions to 1px `#C5A880`. No default browser focus rings; use a faint outer 1px gold hairline ring.
- Labels: Small, floating or pinned top uppercase labels in `label-caps` (`#736B63`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: 16x16px square, 0px radius, 1px `#C5A880` border. Checked state renders a solid gold fill with an ultra-thin white architectural checkmark.
- Radio buttons: 16x16px diamond or sharp square indicator with an inner nested gold square upon selection.

### Directory Special Components
- **Royal Verification Badge:** A miniature gold diamond icon (`12x12px`) enclosing an emerald (`#0F382C`) micro-dot, paired with `label-caps` text reading "SHAHI VERIFIED".
- **Lookbook Carousel Navigator:** Minimal hairline gold arrows (`18px`) enclosed within 44x44px sharp border boxes, positioned symmetrically over media edges.
- **Price Range Indexer:** Expressed through bespoke gold markers (e.g., `✦ ✦ ✦ ✧`) indicating luxury bracket, avoiding standard currency abbreviations where possible.
